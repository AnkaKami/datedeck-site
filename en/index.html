Your ideas hide in the deck

You write your own date from a template: section, title, a couple of lines on what to do, budget and where. It looks exactly like every other card and comes up among them.

  • You decide how often your cards come up: often, sometimes or rarely.
  • You can add an idea even after the gift is opened.
  • If he skips your card, it isn’t lost and will come up again.

Who it’s for

  • For him

    You give the deck to your boyfriend. He opens it with the code on the card, and you get your own page: your secret cards and your album of dates.

  • For us

    You buy the deck for yourself. It’s yours right away, and your girlfriend gets her own code to slip her date ideas into the deck and see your album.

  • For a couple

    A gift from friends for a wedding or anniversary. Two codes: the deck for him, her date ideas and the album for her. You only see whether it’s been opened.

Questions

What does it take to open the gift?

An iPhone and the Date Deck app. He opens the link or scans the QR on the card, or types the code in the app.

Will he know which cards are mine?

No. Your cards look like all the others. He only finds out if you reveal the secret.

Why upload a photo?

The photo shows the date happened. Without it the next card won’t draw. If a card doesn’t work, he can put it back and draw three new ones.

What if the link gets forwarded?

The code works once. As soon as he opens the gift it burns out, and anyone else sees “gift already opened”. If the code is lost, you can issue a new one from your email.

Is there an Android version?

iPhone only for now. Android and Telegram versions will come later.

Who can see our photos?

Only the two of you. Photos are kept in private storage and are never public.
